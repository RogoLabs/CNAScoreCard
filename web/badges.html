CNA Scorecard Badges

Display your CNA Scorecard rating on your website, README, or documentation! Choose from three badge styles and copy the code to embed in your content.

How to Use

  1. Search for your CNA name above
  2. Choose your preferred badge style (Rank, Score, or Combined)
  3. Click the "Copy" button to copy the badge code
  4. Paste the code into your README, website, or documentation

Note: Badges are automatically updated every time the CNA Scorecard pipeline runs, so your badge will always reflect your latest score!

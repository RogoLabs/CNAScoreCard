How is this scored?

CNA Scorecard uses a transparent, percentage-based system to promote quality and accountability in the CVE ecosystem.

Perfect Great Good Needs Work Missing Data

Scores are calculated as a percentage of possible points, then mapped to a grade for clarity. Raw point values are only shown here for transparency.
